/ What I’m doing now

One lead, five moments.

At Dubai Sotheby’s I design the tools agents use every day. Around that work I design the whole lead journey: HubSpot to capture and nurture, AI to take on the busywork, and a person in charge of every decision that matters. Pick a moment to see what I design there.

How AI fits into my process.

I use Claude as a design partner, not a shortcut. It speeds up the slow parts so more of my time goes to judgement, taste and talking to users.

  1. Research synthesisTurning interview notes, tickets and analytics into themes, then checking every theme against the raw source.
  2. UX writing and edge casesDrafting microcopy, error states and empty states, and stress-testing a flow for the branches people forget.
  3. Prototypes in real codeMoving from Figma to working HTML prototypes that stakeholders can click through in a browser on day one.
  4. Design QAReviewing builds for accessibility, consistency with the design system, and copy that drifted from the spec.

Made this way. This portfolio was rebuilt with Claude, from the copy to the particle journey you just scrolled through.
